What happens after confirmation

Your account is disabled while we permanently remove its data from active systems and object storage. Requests are completed within 30 days under our privacy policy.

  • Your profile, location, matching preferences, likes, passes, matches, chat messages, photos, and voice recordings are deleted.
  • Financial transaction records are retained separately for 7 years under our retention policy; your account links and stored payment phone details are removed during erasure.
  • See the privacy policy for the full retention policy and any safety or legal exceptions.

You can also use Profile → Delete account and purge data in the app. Account deletion is different from pausing your profile.
